From the moment audiences step into the venue, the ambient pre-show images and sounds sweeping across [[theatre:Arts Collective Theatre]]'s stunning media wall establish an atmosphere of remembrance and reverence. What unfolds over the next 102 minutes — a runtime precisely honoring the span from the first plane's impact to the fall of the North Tower — is a deeply powerful work of live storytelling.<!--more-->

Written and performed by Jason Nettle, [[production:2026 We Will Never Forget|9/11: We Will Never Forget]] relies on remarkable restraint to deliver immense emotional weight. Downstage of the video projection wall, the staging is strikingly simple: a single chair, a table, an FDNY hat and an old-school telephone. Through the imagery and elements, plus pure performance craft, Nettle breathes life into 22 distinct individuals, drawing from survivor accounts and his own deeply personal history in New York City.

The emotional arc extends beyond the final bows. During the post-show Q&A, the room shared a profound moment of collective catharsis — a necessary release after such an intense journey. Nettle revealed that while tonight featured 22 monologues, his years of research have produced over 70, reflecting an extraordinary commitment to preserving these voices.

For those who have spent time in New York City during commemorative moments, the visual resonance of memory is unforgettable. Yet here in Northeast Florida, this production serves as a vital bridge to that same spirit. It reminds us not only of the tragedy itself, but of the immediate aftermath: a city defined by unity, compassion and shared humanity.

Nettle leaves his audience with a poignant call to action: art is humanity and humanity is art. As we approach this pivotal 25th anniversary, it is a message worth holding close. Whether by revisiting works like *Come From Away*, supporting one of the local theatrical productions opening across the area or simply engaging in thoughtful intergenerational conversations, this unforgettable piece urges us all to remember, connect and heal together.

Audiences have one final chance to experience this powerful piece tomorrow evening, Thursday, at Ponte Vedra High School. Seeing Nettle perform in an educational space centered on community feels uniquely fitting for a work dedicated to memory, empathy and passing these vital stories down to the next generation. Don't miss the opportunity to witness it.
