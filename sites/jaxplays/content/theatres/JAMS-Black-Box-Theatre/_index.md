Founded in 2025, JAMS Black Box Theatre was established as an extension of Jacksonville Arts and Music School to provide a permanent home for theatrical excellence, artistic collaboration and community engagement. What began as an educational performance space quickly evolved into a professional venue where students, educators and local artists create and perform together.

The Black Box Theatre serves as the performance hub for JAMS' theatre education programs while also producing community productions, concerts, special events, workshops and cultural experiences for audiences throughout Northeast Florida. By bringing together youth performers, teaching artists, parents and professional creatives, the theatre bridges arts education and the professional stage.

JAMS Black Box Theatre continues its mission of developing the next generation of artists while enriching Jacksonville's cultural landscape through high-quality, accessible live performance.
