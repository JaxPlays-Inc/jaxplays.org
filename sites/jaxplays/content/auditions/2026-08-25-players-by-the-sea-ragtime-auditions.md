[[theatre:Players by the Sea]] will hold auditions for all roles in [[production:2026 Ragtime]] at noon Saturday, Aug. 29.

Registration will run from 11:30 a.m. to noon, with auditions beginning promptly at noon. Auditions will take place at Players by the Sea, 106 N. 6th St., Jacksonville Beach, FL 32250.

The production is the final show of Players by the Sea's Season 61.

Sweeping and powerful, *Ragtime* paints a portrait of America at the turn of the 20th century, when industrial progress collided with racial and class divides. Through the intersecting stories of an upper-class white family, a Black musician from Harlem, and a Jewish immigrant and his daughter, the musical captures the struggle for justice, freedom and the elusive American Dream.

With its lush score by Stephen Flaherty and Lynn Ahrens, *Ragtime* remains a timeless story of hope, change and the pursuit of equality.

Detailed character descriptions, audition preparation information and registration specifics are available at [playersbythesea.org/auditions](https://playersbythesea.org/auditions).

### Audition Details

- **What:** *Ragtime* auditions
- **When:** Saturday, Aug. 29, at noon
- **Registration:** 11:30 a.m.-noon
- **Where:** [[venue:John McManus Mainstage Theatre|Players by the Sea, 106 N. 6th St., Jacksonville Beach, FL 32250]]
- **Seeking:** All roles
- **More information:** [playersbythesea.org/auditions](https://playersbythesea.org/auditions)
