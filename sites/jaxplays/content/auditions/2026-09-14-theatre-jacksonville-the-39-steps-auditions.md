[[theatre:Theatre Jacksonville]] will hold auditions for [[production:2027 The 39 Steps|The 39 Steps]], adapted by Patrick Barlow from the novel by John Buchan and the movie by Alfred Hitchcock.

Auditions are scheduled for 6:30 p.m. Monday, Oct. 5, at Theatre Jacksonville, 2032 San Marco Blvd., Jacksonville, FL 32207.

The production will be directed by Daniel Austin. Performances are scheduled for Jan. 16-31, 2027.

Auditions will consist of telling a 30-second story or, if preferred, performing a 30-second monologue. Performers should be prepared to present the piece two ways: once normally and again in a heightened film noir style with any European dialect of their choice. Auditions will also include cold reads from the script.

Theatre Jacksonville is seeking one male actor, one female actor and two actors of any gender. All roles require strong comedic timing, physical agility, endurance, a wide range of accents and vocal characterizations, versatility and a strong sense of play.

### Available Roles

**Richard Hannay**  
Age range: 20s to 50s. Richard Hannay is a dapper English gentleman and 1930s leading-man type. The actor must be able to maintain an upper-class British accent, display excellent physical comedy skills and have the stamina to be present throughout the play. This role includes intimacy choreography.

**Annabella Schmidt / Pamela / Margaret**  
Age range: 20s to 50s. One actor plays three distinct roles: a mysterious secret agent, an intelligent woman caught up in Hannay's adventure and a young Scottish farmer's wife dreaming of life beyond the farm. The actor must perform British, German and Scottish accents, display strong comedic skills and create three distinct characterizations. This role includes intimacy choreography.

**Clown #1 and Clown #2**  
Age range: 20s to 50s. These two actors portray more than 150 roles, including heroes, villains, police officers, innkeepers, spies and inanimate objects.

More information is available at [theatrejax.com/auditions](https://www.theatrejax.com/auditions).
