[[theatre:A Classic Theatre]] will hold auditions for [[production:2026 Vanya and Sonia and Masha and Spike]], Christopher Durang's comedy directed by Grace Reed.

Auditions will be held at 11 a.m. Saturday, Sept. 12, and 6:30 p.m. Tuesday, Sept. 15, at [[venue:The Waterworks]], 184 San Marco Ave., in St. Augustine.

Old rivalries, unfulfilled dreams and a potential plan to sell the family estate stir chaos into a dysfunctional trio of siblings, and Spike.

Auditions will consist of readings from the play. Performers should bring headshots and resumes, if available.

The role of Masha has been pre-cast.

Actors who cannot attend but would like to audition may contact [grace@aclassictheatre.org](mailto:grace@aclassictheatre.org?subject=Vanya%20and%20Sonia%20Audition).

More information is available at [aclassictheatre.org/auditions](http://www.aclassictheatre.org/auditions).

Performances are scheduled for Nov. 6, 7, 8, 13, 14 and 15.

### Available Roles

**Vanya**  
Age range: 50-65. Vanya is a middle-aged man who has spent most of his life caring for his family and living in the shadow of his famous sister, Masha. Intelligent, sensitive and deeply frustrated, he feels that life has passed him by. He longs for connection and meaning but is often paralyzed by nostalgia and pessimism. Beneath his complaints is a man who desperately wants to be seen, valued and loved.

**Sonia**  
Age range: 50-65. Sonia is Vanya's adopted sister, a deeply insecure woman who has also devoted her life to caring for others. She feels unattractive, unwanted and incapable of creating a life of her own. Sonia's journey is ultimately about discovering that she may have more life ahead of her than she has allowed herself to imagine.

**Spike**  
Age range: 20-30. Spike is Masha's young, handsome boyfriend, a Hollywood actor whose confidence far exceeds his talent. He is narcissistic, sexually charged, impulsive and largely oblivious to the emotional effect he has on others. Beneath his superficiality, however, there is a certain vulnerability and uncertainty.

**Cassandra**  
Any age. Cassandra is the family's outspoken housekeeper, named after the prophetess from Greek mythology who could foresee the future but was never believed. She is eccentric, dramatic and prone to making dire predictions about what is coming. Her prophecies are both comic and unsettling, and she often sees the emotional truth of the situation more clearly than anyone else.

**Nina**  
Age range: 20-30. Nina is young, energetic, idealistic and emotionally open. She should have energy, curiosity, confidence and a sense that the world is still opening up for her.
