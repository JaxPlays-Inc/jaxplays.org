Five Points Theatre is the historic theatre space at 1028 Park St. in Jacksonville's Riverside and Five Points neighborhood. Opened in 1927 as the Riverside Theatre, the building later operated as the Five Points Theatre and housed River City Playhouse as a live theatre company during the 1980s.
