Friday Musicale is a historic music organization and event campus in Jacksonville's Riverside neighborhood. Founded in 1890, it is one of the South's longest-running music organizations and remains a cultural home for concerts, courses, community gatherings and private events.

The 645 Oak Street Concert Hall is the campus's largest event space, with theater seating for up to 200 guests. The room includes a full stage, strong acoustics, historic architectural character, audio and visual equipment, kitchen access and Steinway concert grand pianos available for rental use.

Parking is available in the rear lot accessed from Peninsular Place, with a secondary lot across Oak Street from the Blue Room and limited daytime street parking. Accessible parking spaces are located next to the main building, and ramp access is available for the main large buildings on campus.
