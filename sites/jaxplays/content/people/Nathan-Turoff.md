Nathan Turoff is a Northeast Florida theatre artist whose credits include work as an actor and director. He has appeared in productions including *Disney's Beauty and the Beast*, *Spamalot*, *Phantom*, *Escape to Margaritaville* and *Hamlet*, and directed The Island Theater's 2026 production of *12 Angry Jurors*.
