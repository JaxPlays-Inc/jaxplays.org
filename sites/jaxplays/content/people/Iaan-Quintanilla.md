Iaan Quintanilla is an actor and vocalist who was born and raised on Guam. He graduated from Douglas Anderson School of the Arts, where he majored in voice. His credits include Agwe in *Once on This Island*, *Peter Pan*, *Blood Brothers* and *City of Angels*.
