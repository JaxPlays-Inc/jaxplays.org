Violinist Philip Pan studied under William Kroll, Felix Galimir, Jens Ellerman and Dorothy DeLay. After receiving his Bachelor of Music and Master of Music degrees in performance from the Juilliard School, he became concertmaster of the Jacksonville Symphony Orchestra and held that position for 32 years.

Pan was a founding member of numerous classical ensembles specializing in Baroque and contemporary music and appeared regularly with the Amelia Island Chamber Music Festival under Artistic Director Christopher Rex. He later expanded his stylistic versatility as a member of indie bands Canary in the Coalmine, Folk is People, the Firewater Tent Revival, the Swell, Mariachi Primera Costa, Patsy's Daydream, and Mote and the Wolf. He has recorded for JJ Grey & Mofro and many other artists.

His musical flexibility embraces genres including rock, country, bluegrass, jazz and mariachi. In 2018, he was named Performing Artist of the Year by the Cultural Council of Greater Jacksonville. Pan and his wife, Cheryl, enjoy travel, cooking, camping, music festivals and their cats, Gypsy and Winky.
