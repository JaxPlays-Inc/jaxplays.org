Praised for her "remarkably beautiful soprano voice and stunning combination of vocal athleticism and theatrical smarts," soprano Brooklyn Snow is a versatile performer in opera and musical theater. In the 2025-26 season, Snow appeared as Lulu in *Elmer Gantry* with Opera Santa Barbara, Sofia in *Il Signor Bruschino* and *Trouble in Tahiti* with [[theatre:Amelia Island Opera]], and made her French debut as Adina in *L'elisir d'amore* with Musica Le Mans. She returns to Amelia Island Opera in 2027 to sing Sophie in Massenet's *Werther*.

Recent highlights include Eliza Doolittle in *My Fair Lady*, Adina in *L'elisir d'amore*, Cinderella in *Into the Woods*, Susanna in *Le nozze di Figaro* and Adele in *Die Fledermaus*. Snow made her Lincoln Center debut at Alice Tully Hall as soprano soloist in the premiere of Dan Forrest's *Jubilate Deo* and has appeared as a soprano soloist at Carnegie Hall and the Italian Consulate in New York with Teatro Nuovo.

Originally from Beaverton, Oregon, Snow holds degrees from the University of Oregon and Indiana University Jacobs School of Music. She lives in Nashville, Tennessee, with her husband, tenor Matthew Greenblatt, and their two daughters.
