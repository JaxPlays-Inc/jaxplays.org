Redgie Gutshall was a fixture of the Northeast Florida theatre community for decades, both onstage and in the front row. A leading man on Jacksonville stages beginning in the 1980s, he studied theatre at Southwest Missouri State College and spent a long career as a U.S. Navy air traffic control officer before making his home in the region's playhouses. Theatre Jacksonville remembered him as "one of Jacksonville's outstanding leading men," but he was known just as widely as a tireless patron — attending productions, encouraging artists and championing companies across the city alongside his wife, [[person:Holly Gutshall]]. He died Sept. 15, 2026, at the age of 79.
