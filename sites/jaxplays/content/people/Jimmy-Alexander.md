Jimmy Alexander's path has been anything but conventional. At 17, he joined the United States Marine Corps, an experience that shaped his discipline, resilience, accountability and commitment to serving something larger than himself. Soon after, he became a father to his daughter, Kayla, at 20, followed by Gavin and Tori. Raising a young family while building a career taught him lasting lessons about responsibility, sacrifice, perseverance and unconditional love.

Throughout his life, Alexander has been drawn equally to science and art. After his military service, he attended Jacksonville University, where he studied music and biology. Music led him to opportunities as a bassoonist, vocalist, performer and artist, while biology ultimately paved the way to a career in dentistry and implant surgery. Along the way, he explored musical theatre, film and music video work, discovering a deep appreciation for storytelling and the ability of the arts to bring people together through shared experiences.

One of the most meaningful chapters of that journey has been his many years studying voice with his friend and mentor Matt Morgan. Professionally, Alexander is the founder of Alexander Dental in St. Augustine, where he has practiced dentistry and implant surgery since 2009. Beyond caring for his patients, he has dedicated countless hours to pro bono service and has worked with many veterans and individuals with disabilities.

Alexander believes the most powerful theatre does not simply entertain; it starts conversations. His life and work continue to reflect a belief in compassion, human connection and showing up for people during life's most challenging moments.
