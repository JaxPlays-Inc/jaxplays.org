Bryce Cofield is a director, designer and special effects artist whose work spans stage and film, with a focus on visually immersive storytelling and bold theatricality. Based in Jacksonville, Florida, Cofield has built a reputation for blending technical innovation with emotionally grounded performances, creating productions that are as striking as they are impactful.

Cofield's professional experience includes work with the Labyrinth Theater Company, where he served as a lighting and sound technician on *Family for All Occasions* under the direction of Philip Seymour Hoffman. This experience helped shape his collaborative approach and deepened his appreciation for actor-driven storytelling supported by precise technical design.

As a director, Cofield has led a range of ambitious productions, including *The Addams Family*, *Jekyll & Hyde the Musical*, *The Great Gatsby* and *Shrek the Musical*. His directing work is known for its cinematic pacing, inventive staging and integration of practical effects to heighten the audience experience.

Cofield has also worked professionally at the Alhambra Theatre & Dining, contributing to productions in both creative and technical capacities. His time at the Arts Center of Coastal Carolina allowed him to expand his range as both an actor and technician, further strengthening his understanding of the full production process from multiple perspectives.

As a designer, Cofield's recent credits include set and lighting design for *Newsies the Musical*, where he crafted a dynamic visual environment that supported high-energy storytelling, as well as lighting design for *Bright Star*, showcasing his ability to create atmosphere and emotional depth through light. In addition to his theater work, he has designed creature effects and practical elements for film, continuing to explore new ways to merge theatrical storytelling with cinematic techniques.
