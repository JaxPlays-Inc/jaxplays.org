Bethany Griffin is making her [[theatre:Amelia Island Opera]] debut alongside her cousin, Victoria Isernia. Based in Staten Island, New York, Griffin holds a BFA in Musical Theatre from SUNY Cortland and is a member of Alpha Psi Omega, the National Theatre Honor Society.

Her recent credits include her 54 Below debut in *Cabaret for the Chronically Dramatic*, the original Off-Broadway musical *In Emily's Words* at Playhouse on 46th, the tri-state tour of *A Christmas Carol* where she served as the female swing, several murder mystery productions with Kill-Her Entertainment, Frenchy in *Sweet Charity* and Marty in *Grease*.
