Ashlyn Scott is active in Jacksonville's theatre community and is appearing as Little Girl in [[production:2026 Ragtime|*Ragtime*]] at Players by the Sea. Her past roles include Ensemble in FSCJ's Summer Musical Theatre Experience production of *The SpongeBob Musical*; Medda Larkin in *Newsies Jr.*, Grandma Addams in *The Addams Family School Edition*, and Juno in *Beetlejuice Jr.* at SET Studios; and Ensemble in *Hadestown* at Greenlight Theatre. A member of the SET Studios Company Team, Ashlyn enjoys bringing stories to life onstage and continuing to learn and grow as a performer.
