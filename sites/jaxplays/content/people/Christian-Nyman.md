Christian Nyman is a Jacksonville music educator and music director. He is the choral director at First Coast High School, holds a Bachelor of Music Education from Florida State University and has music-directed productions for ABET, FSCJ Summer Musical Theatre Experience, Arts Collective Theatre and Greenlight Theatre Company.
