Paul Houghtaling is a bass-baritone, stage director and educator whose career spans opera, concert repertoire and new work. As a performer, he has appeared with companies and ensembles across the United States and Europe, with repertoire ranging from Bach and Handel to contemporary opera. As a director and choreographer, Houghtaling has staged productions for companies including Mobile Opera, Opera Mississippi, Red River Lyric Opera, Opera on the James and Druid City Opera. He serves as Professor and Co-Coordinator of Voice and Director of Opera Theatre at the University of Alabama.
