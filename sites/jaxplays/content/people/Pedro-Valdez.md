Pedro Valdez is a tenor whose work centers on bel canto and lyric repertoire. He has performed with companies including Opera Orlando, Finger Lakes Opera, Northern Lights Music Festival, Madison Opera and Sarasota Opera, with roles that include Rodolfo in *La Boheme*, Rinuccio in *Gianni Schicchi*, Don Ottavio in *Don Giovanni* and Don Ramiro in *La Cenerentola*. Valdez holds degrees from Texas State University and The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley, and has trained as a studio artist with Opera Orlando and Madison Opera.
