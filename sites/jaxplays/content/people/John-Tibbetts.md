Operatic baritone John Tibbetts, praised for his "unexpectedly rich voice" and "reverberating tones" (Palm Beach Post), is building a national performing career spanning companies from Southern California to Western New York while remaining rooted in Northeast Florida as Co-Founder and Artistic Director of [[theatre:Amelia Island Opera]].

Recent engagements include his Pacific Opera Project debut in Los Angeles as Vulcano in the U.S. premiere of Donizetti's *Il fortunato inganno* and his Union Avenue Opera debut in St. Louis as Perchik in *Fiddler on the Roof*. Upcoming appearances include Marco in Puccini's *Gianni Schicchi* with Gulfshore Opera, Figaro in Rossini's *The Barber of Seville* with Opera Mississippi and Papageno in Mozart's *Die Zauberflote* with Nickel City Opera.

Recent operatic highlights include Sharpless in *Madama Butterfly*, Ping in *Turandot* with Opera Grand Rapids, Le Dancaire in *Carmen* with the Richmond Symphony, Masetto in *Don Giovanni* with Opera Tampa and Gulfshore Opera, Marcello in *La boheme*, Ben in Menotti's *The Telephone* and the title role in Rossini's *Il signor Bruschino*.

An active concert soloist, Tibbetts has appeared with the Atlanta Symphony Orchestra and Atlanta Master Chorale, and his repertoire includes Haydn's *The Creation*, Brahms's *Ein deutsches Requiem*, Britten's *War Requiem* and Orff's *Carmina Burana*. Internationally, he made his operatic film debut as Adam in Edwin Huizinga's *Angel* with Toronto's Opera Atelier.

At Amelia Island Opera, Tibbetts works alongside Executive Director Victoria Isernia in shaping the company's artistic growth throughout Northeast Florida. He recently oversaw its record-breaking fifth season, which included five consecutive sold-out productions and the launch of the Amelia Island Opera Vocal Competition.
