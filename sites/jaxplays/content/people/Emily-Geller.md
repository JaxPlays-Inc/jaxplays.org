NYC-based contralto Emily Geller has been praised for her "lower extension that has to be heard to be believed" (Seen and Heard International) and for being "hilariously over the top" (Opera Magazine). In 2026, she made her Opera Modesto debut, reprising Jezibaba in *Rusalka*, and returns to Salt Marsh Opera in the same role.

Geller joined Beth Morrison Projects in *Monna Innominata* at National Sawdust as part of NEXTGEN, and made another company debut with Utah Festival Opera & Musical Theatre, performing Counsel for the Plaintiff in *Trial by Jury* and returning to the role of Ruth in *The Pirates of Penzance*. Her portrayal of Ruth was praised as "comic gold" and for its "clear and beautiful" vocals.

She stars as Miss Todd in *The Old Maid and the Thief* with [[theatre:Amelia Island Opera]], marking her fourth company debut of the 2026 season. Upcoming 2027 engagements include Pacific Opera Project as Suzy in *La rondine*, Gulfshore Opera as Zita in *Gianni Schicchi* and Countess in *Pique Dame* with Opera Modesto.
