Ella Escamilla has trained at Cincinnati Conservatory of Music, Oklahoma City University and BAA in New York. She has received scholarships and awards, including the Andrew Lloyd Webber Training Scholarship and Applause Award nominations.

Her credits include *Hadestown* as Eurydice at Douglas Anderson School of the Arts, as well as Limelight Theatre productions of *Parade* as Mary Phagan, *Little Women* as Young Amy and *Little Miss Sunshine* as Miss California.
