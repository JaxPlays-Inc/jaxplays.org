Austin Bailly is a third-year student at the University of Florida, where he is pursuing a BFA in musical theatre. His recent credits include *Some Enchanted Evening* at the Hippodrome Theatre, as well as University of Florida productions of *Rent* as Angel and *The Music Man* as male swing.

He is deeply appreciative of his family and friends for their continued love and support. You can follow his journey on Instagram [@austin.bailly](https://instagram.com/austin.bailly).
