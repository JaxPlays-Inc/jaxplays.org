Mark Covey is a bass-baritone, director and classical guitarist originally from Monterrey, Mexico. He has performed across the United States and in Germany, France and Italy, with roles including Escamillo in *Carmen*, Rambaldo in *La rondine*, Bartolo in *Le nozze di Figaro*, Papageno in *Die Zauberflote* and Eisenstein in *Die Fledermaus*. Covey holds music degrees from Pepperdine University and the University of California, Santa Barbara, and serves as Assistant Professor of Voice at Angelo State University.
