Sherrie Provence is the Producing Artistic Director of [[theatre:Catalyst Theatre]], a St. Augustine company committed to professional theatre and storytelling that inspires, emboldens and strengthens the cultural life of the community. She previously served as executive director of the [[theatre:Limelight Theatre]].

Provence is a SAG-AFTRA and AEA actor with an MFA in Acting and a BA in Theatre. She has acted and directed on stages in London, Scotland, Russia, Ukraine, Germany and Austria, and has worked with producers, directors and writers on original work for Broadway and off-Broadway. Her career also includes work as an actor, director, writer, producer and professor of theatre.

She founded three theatre companies and a production company in Atlanta, Georgia, where her work connected with Turner Broadcasting, A&E Network and 20th Century Fox. Her film and television credits include work alongside artists such as Donald Glover, Tyler Perry, Tony Hale, Jim Carrey and Clint Eastwood. Through Catalyst Theatre, Provence describes her current chapter as a return to theatre as a force for hope, laughter, community and meaningful change.
