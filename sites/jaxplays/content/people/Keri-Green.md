Keri Green is an actor who began performing at age 3 and has studied with Tony Award-winning actors in New York as well as acting coaches in Los Angeles. Her favorite roles include Gwendolyn Fairfax in *The Importance of Being Earnest*, Judy Martin in *Home, I'm Darling* and Linoleum in *The Great American Trailer Park Musical*.
