Anthony Offerle, bass baritone, boasts a dramatic presence and has appeared with companies including Opera Estate in Rome, Cincinnati Opera, Opera Mississippi, International Chamber Orchestra, Dayton Opera, First Coast Opera, Salt Marsh Opera, Shreveport Opera, Wyoming Opera, Intermountain Opera, Pacific Opera, Italy's Festival of International Opera and Spoleto Festival USA.

Offerle has sung more than 30 leading roles, including the title roles in *Don Giovanni* and *Don Pasquale*, Scarpia in *Tosca*, Don Alfonso in *Cosi fan tutte*, Papageno in *Die Zauberflote*, Don Magnifico and Alidoro in *La Cenerentola*, and Bartolo in both *Il barbiere di Siviglia* and *Le nozze di Figaro*.

Equally at home with operetta and musical theater, Offerle has performed as the Major General in *The Pirates of Penzance*, Poo Bah in *The Mikado*, Sir Despard Murgatroyd in *Ruddigore*, Riff in *West Side Story* and Curly in *Oklahoma*. He created the role of Muffeo in the world premiere of Stella Sung's opera *The Red Silk Thread: An Epic Tale of Marco Polo*.

Offerle is a two-time Metropolitan Opera auditions district winner and regional finalist, a national winner of the Federation of Music Clubs vocal competition and one of ten Americans selected to compete in the Marian Anderson International Vocal Competition. He serves as Associate Professor of Voice at the University of Florida in Gainesville.
