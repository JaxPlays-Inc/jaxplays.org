Lauren Manacup is an actor and dancer whose credits include Sandy in *Grease*, Angie in *The Prom*, *Shrek*, *Escape to Margaritaville*, *Mary Poppins* and *The Addams Family*. She is also a former NFL cheerleader and works as a Trust & Estate Coordinator.
