Italian-American soprano Victoria Isernia is celebrated for her rich, sultry voice, praised for her "good acting instincts" and described as "engaging to watch" (Utah Theatre Bloggers). Equally accomplished in opera and musical theatre, Isernia maintains a diverse repertoire spanning both genres.

In the 2026-2027 season, Isernia joins Teatro Lirico d'Europa and the NJ Association of Verismo Opera as Zerlina in *Don Giovanni*, and makes her role debut as Charlotte in *Werther* with [[theatre:Amelia Island Opera]]. Recent highlights include touring with Teatro Lirico d'Europa in the title role of *Carmen*, her mainstage debut with First Coast Opera as Pitti-Sing in *The Mikado* and role debuts as Dinah in *Trouble in Tahiti* and Judith in *Bluebeard's Castle*. Other recent roles include Musetta in *La boheme*, Hansel in *Hansel & Gretel* and Mrs. Nolan in *The Medium*.

From 2020-2023, Isernia was a Resident Artist with Utah Festival Opera & Musical Theatre, where she performed numerous leading roles, including her acclaimed 2022 role debut as Carmen. Her musical theatre credits include Belle in *Beauty & the Beast*, Kim in *Bye Bye Birdie*, Velma Von Tussle in *Hairspray*, Rona in *The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee*, Ilona in *She Loves Me* and Sarah in Sondheim's *Company*.

A frequent interpreter of Gilbert & Sullivan, Isernia has performed roles including Pitti-Sing in *The Mikado*, Lady Jane in *Patience* and Tessa in *The Gondoliers*. She made her Lincoln Center debut in 2013 with the New York Philharmonic in Beethoven's Ninth Symphony. An alumna of the Manhattan School of Music, Isernia studied under Maitland Peters and performed leading roles there including Dido in *Dido & Aeneas* and Nerone in *L'incoronazione di Poppea*.
