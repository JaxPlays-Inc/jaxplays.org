Jonathan Chapman is a choreographer, dancer, teacher and performing artist on the creative team of Catalyst Theatre. Raised in Atlanta, Georgia, he began dance training at age 13, studying ballet, modern, contemporary, jazz and tap before earning a full scholarship to Belhaven University's dance department in Jackson, Mississippi.

Chapman continued his training in Ballet Magnificat's Professional Training Program and was promoted first to apprentice in 2008 and then to Company Artist from 2009-14. His classical and contemporary ballet career has taken him across much of the continental United States and to 15 countries, with performances including the International Ballet Festival in Barranquilla, Colombia, and work with Suzhou Ballet Theatre in Suzhou, China.

He has appeared as a guest artist and instructor with companies and programs including Shechah Touring Company in Japan, Project Dance, Boca Ballet Theatre, Alabama Dance Theatre, At Marah Dance Theatre in Russia and Poland, and Roswell Dance Theatre. In 2014, Chapman and his wife founded The Perla Ballet in St. Augustine with Artistic Director Lori Ladwig, where he helps students pursue technical excellence and creative expression. He also works as a master class teacher, choreographer and guest performing artist.
