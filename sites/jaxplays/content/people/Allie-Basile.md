Allie Basile is a young actor from North Florida who began performing in community theater at age 7. She has appeared in more than 20 musicals, primarily at Amelia Musical Playhouse and other community theaters. Favorite roles include Cindy Brady in *The Bardy Bunch* and the title role in *Annie*, both at Amelia Musical Playhouse.
