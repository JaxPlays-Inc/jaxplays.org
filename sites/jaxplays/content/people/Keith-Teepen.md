Keith Teepen has performed both as a soloist and collaborative pianist in Europe, Asia, South America and throughout North America. He is a multifaceted musician who has collaborated with a diverse spectrum of celebrated artists, including American operatic tenor Bryan Hymel, trumpeter Tine Thing Helseth and Broadway performers Laura Osnes and Donna Vivino.

As a soloist and chamber musician, Teepen has performed with his piano duo partner, Nariaki Sugiura, the Red River Trio and other artists around the world. His training and passion for collaboration in musical theatre and opera have led to an abundance of performance opportunities. His music-directing credits include *The Last Five Years*, *Godspell*, *James and the Giant Peach*, *I Love You, You're Perfect, Now Change*, *Nunsense* and others. In the opera world, he frequently performs with First Coast Opera and served as the official pianist for the Metropolitan Opera National Council Auditions' North Dakota-Manitoba District from 2015-18.

Teepen often provides arrangements and transcriptions for prominent artists in both vocal and instrumental repertoire. In 2019, he recorded the album *Duality: Works for Tenor Saxophone & Piano* with saxophonist Scott Sandberg. The album, published by Albany Records in February 2020, features an eclectic collection of works, some of which were commissioned for Sandberg. He has performed piano duo recitals with Sugiura throughout Asia and the United States.

In addition to his performance schedule, Teepen is a respected educator. He has served on the piano faculty at Pro-Musica Music Festival in Juiz de Fora, Brazil, and has held collaborative piano positions at DePauw University School of Music, Indiana University Jacobs School of Music and the University of North Dakota. He has taught master classes in both solo and collaborative piano in New York, Macau, Hong Kong, Brazil, and cities across the United States and China. He has also lectured on the importance of collaborative pianists in Asia and the United States.

Teepen received his Doctor of Musical Arts degree in collaborative piano from the University of Colorado Boulder and holds Master of Music and Bachelor of Music degrees in piano performance. He is the Tom Kenan assistant professor of piano at Flagler College in St. Augustine, Florida.
