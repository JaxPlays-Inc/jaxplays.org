Tenor Dylan Elza, a Dallas, Texas native, most recently performed as the Noble in Wagner's *Lohengrin* with The Dallas Opera. Last season he portrayed Gabe in *Next to Normal* with Paradox Opera, performed with [[theatre:Amelia Island Opera]] as Il Commissario in *Il Signor Bruschino* and Trio #2 in *Trouble in Tahiti*, and sang in The Dallas Opera's Education and Outreach production. He will perform next as Romeo in Gounod's *Romeo et Juliette* with Opera Arlington.

Additional recent highlights include Giuseppe in Verdi's *La traviata* with The Dallas Opera, Don Ottavio in *Don Giovanni* with Opera Arlington, Gherardo in *Gianni Schicchi* with Opera on the James, Gaston in *Beauty and the Beast* at Firehouse Theater and Le Prince Charmant in Viardot's *Cendrillon* with Opera Arlington.

His opera programs include Opera on the James, Florida Grand Opera, Lakes Area Music Festival, Central City Opera, Virginia Opera, Charlottesville Opera, Opera Saratoga, the Young Artist Vocal Academy at Houston Grand Opera and Seagle Music Colony.
