Clayton Riddley is a music educator and performer. He teaches music at KIPP VOICE Academy and was named the school's 2026-27 Teacher of the Year. His stage credits include *Snoopy*, *Bonnie and Clyde*, *Hairspray*, *Violet*, *The Toxic Avenger*, *Memphis*, *Working*, *The Color Purple*, *Romeo and Juliet*, *Sweeney Todd*, *Twelfth Night* and *You're a Good Man, Charlie Brown*. He is a member of Phi Mu Alpha and a founding member of [Sweet Tea Shakespeare](https://sweetteashakespeare.com/).
