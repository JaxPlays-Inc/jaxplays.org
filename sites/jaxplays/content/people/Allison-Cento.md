Allison Cento is a junior majoring in Theatre Arts with a minor in Music. She has appeared in Flagler College’s mainstage productions, including The Thanksgiving Play and James and the Giant Peach, and performs with Cabaret, Key of Seas, and the new Chamber Choir.

Connected to the arts from a young age, Allison has participated in television programs, performed her original music, and represented her community at significant events such as the Oath of Office Ceremony at the Orange County Convention Center, where she received the Hispanic Youth Leadership Award. She continues to pursue her artistic path with dedication and gratitude.
