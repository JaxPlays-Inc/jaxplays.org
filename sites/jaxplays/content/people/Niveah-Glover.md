Niveah Glover is a Jacksonville playwright, poet and spoken-word artist. A Douglas Anderson School of the Arts alum and Howard University student, Glover has received national recognition for writing and oratory, including honors as a U.S. Presidential Scholar in the Arts and Poetry Out Loud national champion.
