Heather Quinn is an actor whose favorite past roles include Princess Fiona in *Shrek: The Musical*, Inga in *Young Frankenstein*, Luisa in *The Fantasticks*, Casey in *First Date*, Linda in *The Wedding Singer* and Shelly/Annie in *Evil Dead: The Musical*. She has also performed as a featured soloist with Central Florida Community Arts in productions including *Wish Upon a Star: A Disney Revue* and *The Magic of Disney*.
