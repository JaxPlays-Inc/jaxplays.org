Mark Dougherty is a collaborative pianist active in the Jacksonville and St. Augustine area. He serves as staff accompanist for Flagler College's music and theatre arts programs, and as pianist and organist for Our Lady of Good Council Catholic Church.

Dougherty regularly accompanies musical theatre productions for Limelight Theatre and has performed as a chamber music pianist at the University of North Florida. He studied classical and collaborative piano at the Florida State University College of Music after graduating from St. Augustine High School.
