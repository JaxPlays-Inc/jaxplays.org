Blake Michael Osner spent his childhood in the theater. At 6, to address a significant speech impediment, his parents placed him in private acting classes. To say he took to this like a fish in water would be an understatement. Within the first year, he advanced into a high school-level class, and by 8 he had auditioned for and been cast in a community theater production of *Stop the World - I Want to Get Off* with an all-adult cast.

Growing up in a city with three faith-based universities gave Osner plentiful opportunities to perform with and learn from adult actors. He is a well-recognized singer, actor, director and producer in the Northeast Florida theater scene. While he spends most of his theater time on stage, he also loves the directing process and collaborating with other artists to bring stories to life. A lifelong learner, he is always excited to work with and learn from casts and production teams to create art and tell a story.
