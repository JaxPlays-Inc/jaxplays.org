Craig Reagor is a graduate of the Atlanta Institute of Music and has spent many years teaching and performing throughout the Jacksonville and Atlanta areas. He currently performs with the original projects Cumbe Sonico and The Gilmore Quandary, has appeared as a guest artist aboard Virgin Voyages, and has performed in musical theater productions in the Atlanta area.
