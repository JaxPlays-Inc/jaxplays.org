Anthony Rodriguez is a voice-over director for video games, audiobooks, and English-language film and television dubs. His credits include the English dubs of Nikolaj Arcel's *The Promised Land*, Jason Yu's *Sleep*, and Yang Li's *Escape from the 21st Century*. Outside his work, he enjoys video games, board games, opera, and singing.
