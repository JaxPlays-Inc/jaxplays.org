Tenor Isaac Hurtado is acclaimed for a voice of burnished warmth and ringing brilliance, distinguished by its Italianate depth, vibrant squillo and compelling stage presence. His repertoire centers on the core lyric and spinto tenor canon, with roles including Don Jose in *Carmen*, Pinkerton in *Madama Butterfly*, Riccardo in *Un ballo in maschera*, Werther in *Werther*, Tamino in *Die Zauberflote*, Alfredo in *La traviata*, Rodolfo in *La boheme*, Edgardo in *Lucia di Lammermoor* and Romeo in *Romeo et Juliette*.

During the 2025-26 season, Hurtado made company debuts with Winter Opera St. Louis as Riccardo in *Un ballo in maschera*, Opera Tampa as Tamino in *Die Zauberflote* and Maryland Opera as Pinkerton in *Madama Butterfly*. He also appeared as Alfredo in *La traviata* with Utah Opera and Orquesta Filarmonica del Estado de Chihuahua, and joined Opera Las Vegas as tenor soloist in *Passion for Puccini*.

In the 2026-27 season, he appears as Pinkerton in *Madama Butterfly* with Opera Orlando and sings the title role in *Werther* with [[theatre:Amelia Island Opera]]. Hurtado spent two seasons as Principal Artist in Residence with Opera San Jose, received young artist training with Central City Opera, Opera North and Utah Festival Opera, and holds music degrees from Florida State University, the University of Cincinnati College-Conservatory of Music and Brigham Young University.
