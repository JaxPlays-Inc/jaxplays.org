Sebastian Miller is making his operatic debut with [[theatre:Amelia Island Opera]]. His musical theatre credits include Don Lockwood in *Singin' in the Rain*, Thomas Jefferson in *1776* and Rocky in *Damn Yankees*.

When he is not acting, Miller enjoys writing and performing music with his folk duo Sons of Doctors. He is the fourth of six children and uses performance as an outlet for recouping attention he missed as a middle child. When there is no one to entertain, he reaches for writers including Steinbeck, Orwell and F. Scott Fitzgerald.
