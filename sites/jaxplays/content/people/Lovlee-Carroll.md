Lovlee Carroll is an actor, singer, producer and writer with stage, television and film credits. She grew up in Hanover, Indiana, and earned double bachelor's degrees in theatre and psychology from Indiana University, with musical training through the Jacobs School of Music.

Carroll earned her Actors' Equity card covering Mimi in *Rent* at Westchester Broadway Theatre. Her regional and touring credits include L.A. Theatre Works' national tour of *Seven*, *Around the World in 80 Days*, North Coast Rep, The Human Race Theatre Company and Brown County Playhouse.

On screen, Carroll is known for work on *Grown-ish*, *Grey's Anatomy*, *Scorpion*, *How to Get Away with Murder*, *Rebel*, *The Mindy Project* and three seasons as Lily Bagha in *The New Adventures of Peter and Wendy*. Her film credits include *The Last Conception*, *Await the Dawn* and *Candy Corn*.
