Christen May Mayer is a lyric coloratura soprano based in Oklahoma and Florida whose work spans opera, music theatre and arts outreach. She earned a Master of Music in Opera Performance from Oklahoma City University and has performed roles including Pamina in *Die Zauberflote*, Berta in *Il barbiere di Siviglia*, Clotilde in *Norma* and Sieglinde in *Die Walkure*. Mayer is also active as an educational artist, bringing opera and musical theatre programs to schools and community audiences.
