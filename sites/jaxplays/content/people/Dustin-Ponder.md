Dustin Ponder is an actor whose Theatre Jacksonville credits include Prince Richard in *The Lion in Winter*. He attended the University of Florida, where he studied literature, writing and poetry. Offstage, he is a member of Teamsters Local 512, a longtime union activist, an advocate for workers' rights and a co-founder of The Jax Film Club.
