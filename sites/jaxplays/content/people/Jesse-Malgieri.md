Baritone Jesse Malgieri has enjoyed an international performing career for more than a decade. In the United States, he has appeared with opera companies in Santa Fe, North Carolina, Princeton, Caramoor, Sarasota, Central City, Harlem and the Castleton Festival. Overseas, his engagements include principal roles with the Munich Philharmonic in Germany, Orquesta Sinfonica de Galicia in Spain, Japan Society Opera in Tokyo and Kyrenia Opera in Cyprus.

In addition to his operatic credits, Malgieri has established himself in musical theater repertoire, performing the title role in *Sweeney Todd*, Poo-Bah in *The Mikado*, Count Carl-Magnus in *A Little Night Music* and Don Quixote in *Man of La Mancha*, among others. He has sung with conductors including Lorin Maazel, Harry Bickett, Pablo Heras-Casado, Daniela Candillari, Raymond Leppard, Ken-David Masur and Timothy Myers.

Malgieri has received career grants from the Metropolitan Opera National Council, the Gerda Lissner Foundation, Sarasota Opera and the Career Bridges Foundation.
