Matt Morgan has the distinction of making debuts at three of the four theaters at New York's Lincoln Center. His New York debut came as Don Gomez in Weber's *Die drei Pintos* with Leon Botstein and the American Symphony Orchestra. The same season, he made his New York City Opera debut in the world premiere of Charles Wuorinen's *Haroun and the Sea of Stories*. He later debuted with the National Chorale at Avery Fisher Hall as the tenor soloist in Handel's *Messiah*, and his Kennedy Center debut came in *Das Klagende Lied* with the Washington Chorus.

Since those debuts, Morgan has been a frequent return guest as a principal tenor with New York City Opera and the National Chorale. He created the leading role of Charles Drouet for the world premiere recording of Aldridge and Garfein's *Sister Carrie*, named one of the top five new works of 2018 by *Opera News*. He has also appeared in multiple PBS television specials, including *Matt Morgan in Concert*, *Opera Masters* and *Opera Louisiane*.

Recent and upcoming engagements include a return to Opera Theater of Connecticut for his role debut as Eisenstein in *Die Fledermaus*, a return to First Coast Opera for Don Jose in *Carmen*, the Sanibel Island Music Festival for an Andrew Lloyd Webber concert, and Bach's *Magnificat* with Music at Holy Trinity. Other recent seasons have included appearances with Mississippi Opera, FestivalSouth, Natchez Music Festival, First Coast Opera, Sarasota Key Chorale, the Fort Myers Mastersingers, the Crested Butte Music Festival, Broadway Bangkok, the University of Florida and Louisiana State University.

In addition to his performing schedule, Morgan continues to expand his work as an educator and vocal director. He is senior lecturer of musical theatre voice at the University of Florida, music and vocal director for Apex Theatre Studio, vocal director of Broadway Bangkok and CEO of Vocal Fingerprint LLC. His students can be seen on Broadway, on national tours and in regional productions. He has served as vocal director for 20 musicals and as a lead clinician for musical theatre programs and intensives across the country.
