Evan Bowen is a Jacksonville native who has been involved in theater for 12 years. He earned an Associate of Science degree in musical theater from FloArts. After years of performing on stage, Bowen had the opportunity to apply his guitar skills to the pit orchestra. Since then, he has split time between performing on and off stage.

His credits include playing for Limelight Theatre's production of *Godspell*. He is grateful to his wife, Clare, for her support and to the theaters that continue to make live music a priority in their productions.
