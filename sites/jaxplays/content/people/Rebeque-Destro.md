Rebeque Destro is an actor with a long relationship to the stage, including community, university and professional productions across the United States. Her favorite roles include Queen Eleanor in *The Lion in Winter*, Hannah Jarvis in *Arcadia* at Theater Iowa, Reagan in *King Lear* with Ad Hoc Theater in Denver and Maggie in a U.S. tour of *A Chorus Line*.
