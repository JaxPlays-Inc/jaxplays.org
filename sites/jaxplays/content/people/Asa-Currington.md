Asa Currington is a passionate, skilled craftsman with more than 20 years of experience in woodworking and custom building. A true lover of the arts, he brings a safety-first approach to his work and is a proud supporter of local and small theater productions.

Currington has been a behind-the-scenes friend to many performers and to the theater community. His role as the operator of the Bingo Board control panel in Limelight Theatre's production of *Bingo* was his first chance to participate backstage during a live production. During one packed weekend performance, when an electrical issue arose, he solved it just in time for curtain.
