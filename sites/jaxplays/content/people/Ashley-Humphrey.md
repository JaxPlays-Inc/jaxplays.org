Ashley Humphrey is a dancer and performer whose training began at LaVilla School of the Arts and continued at Douglas Anderson School of the Arts and Florida State College at Jacksonville. Her background includes ballet, pointe, modern, contemporary, jazz, tap, Latin dance styles and pole fitness. She has performed and choreographed for Jacksonville music artists and co-founded Heelz Therapy.
