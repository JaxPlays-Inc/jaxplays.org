Alexis Elza "was Carmen down to her fingernails: seductive and inconstant, with a sultry voice to match" (Cleveland Classical, 2016). She began her operatic career with Kentucky Opera during the 2011-2012 season, appearing in *Carmen* and *The Merry Widow*. Since then, she has performed nationally and internationally, including her solo debut at opening ceremonies at the Peace Palace in The Hague and leading roles such as Helene in *La belle Helene*, Carmen in *Tragedie de Carmen* and Elle in *La voix humaine*.

Elza holds a Master of Music degree from Northwestern University and spent the season following her graduate studies living and studying in Salzburg, Austria. A regular with The Dallas Opera, she performs in the company's chorus as well as in its touring production of John Davies' *The Bremen Town Musicians* as Barcarolle.

In addition to her performing career, Elza is a dedicated educator and music director. She runs a private voice studio and has music directed productions including *Bonnie & Clyde* at Rockwall Community Playhouse and *West Side Story* with Rockwall Summer Musicals.
