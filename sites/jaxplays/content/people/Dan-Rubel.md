Dan Rubel is a professional bassist, arranger, band leader, and cruise ship performer with a Bachelor of Arts in Jazz Performance from the University of North Florida. For more than three decades, he has entertained audiences worldwide, delivering exceptional performances on cruise ships, at corporate events, weddings, and private engagements.
