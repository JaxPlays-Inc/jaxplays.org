*Catch Me If You Can* turns the real-life exploits of Frank Abagnale Jr. into a stylish musical chase, following a restless young man who remakes himself as a pilot, doctor and lawyer while FBI agent Carl Hanratty follows the trail of forged checks and false identities. With a swinging score by Marc Shaiman and Scott Wittman and a book by Terrence McNally, the show balances showbiz sparkle with the ache of a son trying to outrun a broken home.
