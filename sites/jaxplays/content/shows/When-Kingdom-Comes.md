Following the death of legendary blues musician Cornell "Titan" King, five estranged siblings gather at their father's Florida estate to read his will. Written by [[person:Niveah Glover]] *When Kingdom Comes* blends Southern Gothic storytelling, Black folklore, Greek mythology and original music.
