At a silent retreat in the woods, six urban strangers try to find sanctuary, clarity and healing under the watch of an unseen teacher. Bess Wohl's *Small Mouth Sounds* uses long stretches without dialogue to explore grief, desire, awkwardness and the ache of trying to connect when words fail.
