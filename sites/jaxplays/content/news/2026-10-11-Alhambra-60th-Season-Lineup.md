[[theatre:Alhambra Theatre and Dining|Alhambra Theatre & Dining]] will mark its 60th season in 2027 with an eight-show lineup that includes Broadway favorites, classic musicals, comedies and family titles.<!--more-->

The Jacksonville dinner theater announced the season Oct. 8, opening single-show ticket sales and season partnerships for a milestone year that begins Jan. 7 with *Shout! The Mod Musical* and concludes Dec. 24 with *It's a Wonderful Life*.

"This is a milestone that very few theaters get to celebrate," Alhambra Managing Partner Craig Smith said in the announcement. "For 60 seasons, people have come through these doors to laugh, sing, celebrate birthdays and anniversaries, have dinner with friends and experience the magic of live theater."

The 2027 season is built around a mix of nostalgia, popular film-to-stage adaptations and long-running musical theater staples. After *Shout! The Mod Musical*, the Alhambra will stage Dolly Parton's *9 to 5 The Musical*, the Southern comedy *The Hallelujah Girls*, Rodgers & Hammerstein's *South Pacific*, Disney's *Frozen*, *Tootsie*, *The Addams Family* and *It's a Wonderful Life*.

Smith said the anniversary season was designed to honor the theater's history without turning the year into a retrospective.

"This lineup is about what the Alhambra has always done best - putting great entertainment on stage and giving people a reason to come together," Smith said.

### A Milestone Season

The Alhambra first opened in 1967 and remains one of Jacksonville's most recognizable live entertainment institutions. Since Smith and his partners purchased the theater in 2009, the company has grown from an approximately $2 million operation into a business of more than $10 million, according to the announcement, welcoming more than 135,000 guests annually.

The theater also reported that recent seasons have played to near-capacity audiences, with more than 90 percent of available tickets sold. In 2025, *USA TODAY* readers named the Alhambra the No. 1 dinner theater in America in the publication's 10Best Readers' Choice Awards.

"Sixty years doesn't happen because of a building or a show title," Smith said. "It happens because a community decides, year after year, that this place matters."

Several Alhambra team members have worked with the theater for more than three decades, connecting the current company to generations of Jacksonville audiences.

"We have guests who first came here as children and now bring their children and grandchildren," Smith said. "We have employees who have spent decades here. That history belongs to Jacksonville as much as it belongs to us. This is our 60th season, but it's Jacksonville's celebration."

### 2027 Season

- *Shout! The Mod Musical*, Jan. 7-Feb. 7
- Dolly Parton's *9 to 5 The Musical*, Feb. 18-March 28
- *The Hallelujah Girls*, April 1-25
- Rodgers & Hammerstein's *South Pacific*, April 29-June 6
- Disney's *Frozen*, June 10-Aug. 8
- *Tootsie*, Aug. 19-Sept. 26
- *The Addams Family*, Sept. 30-Nov. 14
- *It's a Wonderful Life*, Nov. 18-Dec. 24
