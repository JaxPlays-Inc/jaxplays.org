The [[theatre:Alhambra Theatre and Dining|Alhambra Theatre & Dining]] has announced its 2027 season, an eight-show lineup that will mark the Jacksonville dinner theater's 60th year.<!--more-->

The theater announced the season to subscribers Oct. 1, inviting guests to become Season Partners before single-show seats go on sale to the public Oct. 8.

The season begins Jan. 7, 2027, with [[production:2027 SHOUT! The Mod Musical|SHOUT! The Mod Musical]], a 1960s pop revue built around songs including "Downtown," "Son of a Preacher Man" and "These Boots Are Made for Walkin'." The production runs through Feb. 7.

From there, the Alhambra moves into [[production:2027 9 to 5: The Musical|9 to 5: The Musical]], running Feb. 18-March 28. Inspired by the 1980 film and featuring music and lyrics by Dolly Parton, the workplace comedy follows three women who turn the tables on their sexist boss.

The spring slate continues with [[production:2027 The Hallelujah Girls|The Hallelujah Girls]], April 1-25, a Southern comedy from Jessie Jones, Nicholas Hope and Jamie Wooten about a group of women who transform an abandoned church into a day spa. Rodgers and Hammerstein's [[production:2027 South Pacific|South Pacific]] follows April 29-June 6, bringing the classic World War II-era musical back to the Alhambra stage.

For summer, the theater will present [[production:2027 Disney's Frozen|Disney's Frozen]] from June 10-Aug. 8. The stage adaptation of the animated film features Anna, Elsa and songs including "Let It Go" and "Do You Want to Build a Snowman?"

The season continues with [[production:2027 Tootsie|Tootsie]], Aug. 19-Sept. 26, the fast-paced musical comedy about a struggling actor whose elaborate disguise leads to unexpected Broadway success.

Fall brings [[production:2027 The Addams Family|The Addams Family]], Sept. 30-Nov. 14, following Wednesday Addams, her very normal boyfriend and one deeply uncomfortable family dinner.

The 60th season closes with [[production:2027 It's a Wonderful Life|It's a Wonderful Life]], Nov. 18-Dec. 24, returning George Bailey's story of family, friendship and second chances to the holiday season.

Founded in 1967, the Alhambra is widely recognized as the longest continuously operating professional dinner theater in the United States. The theater was also named the nation's No. 1 dinner theater by readers of *USA TODAY* earlier this year.

The theater is inviting guests to become Season Partners by Oct. 7, 2026, before single-show seats go on sale to the public Oct. 8. Season Partner benefits include early seat selection, season pricing and early access to added programming such as Alhambra After Dark performances.

**2027 season lineup**
- **Jan. 7-Feb. 7:** [[production:2027 SHOUT! The Mod Musical|SHOUT! The Mod Musical]]
- **Feb. 18-March 28:** [[production:2027 9 to 5: The Musical|9 to 5: The Musical]]
- **April 1-25:** [[production:2027 The Hallelujah Girls|The Hallelujah Girls]]
- **April 29-June 6:** [[production:2027 South Pacific|South Pacific]]
- **June 10-Aug. 8:** [[production:2027 Disney's Frozen|Disney's Frozen]]
- **Aug. 19-Sept. 26:** [[production:2027 Tootsie|Tootsie]]
- **Sept. 30-Nov. 14:** [[production:2027 The Addams Family|The Addams Family]]
- **Nov. 18-Dec. 24:** [[production:2027 It's a Wonderful Life|It's a Wonderful Life]]
- **Season Partner deadline:** Oct. 7, 2026
- **Single-show seats:** On sale beginning Oct. 8, 2026

Season information and tickets are available at [alhambrajax.com](https://www.alhambrajax.com).
