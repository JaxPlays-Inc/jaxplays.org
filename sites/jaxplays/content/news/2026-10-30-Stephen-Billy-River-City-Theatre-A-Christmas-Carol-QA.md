As the holiday season approaches, [[theatre:River City Theatre]] is bringing a fresh spark to Downtown Jacksonville's arts scene with Patrick Barlow's adaptation of *A Christmas Carol*, blending humor with heart.<!--more-->

JaxPlays spoke with River City Theatre Executive Artistic Director [[person:Stephen Billy]] about nostalgia, comedy and the wonder of storytelling in the upcoming production.

*This interview has been edited for length and clarity.*

**JaxPlays: What inspired the selection of this particular adaptation of a literary classic for your holiday window?**

**Stephen Billy:** My deep affection for *A Christmas Carol* began years ago when I played Peter Cratchit onstage. This specific adaptation breathes vibrant new life into a timeless story, offering a fresh perspective that resonates with audiences of all ages. No amount of rereading the novella or revisiting various film versions ever dims the magic of these characters and their journey.

What makes this version truly special is its brilliant blend of physical comedy and heartfelt drama, courtesy of playwright Patrick Barlow, providing our actors with incredible opportunities for inventive character work.

**JaxPlays: How would you describe your overarching artistic vision for this staging?**

**Billy:** Our production approaches the staging through the lens of storytelling, with the actors stepping directly onto the cobblestone streets of London. They creatively utilize everyday found objects and props to create the environment and bring the tale to life, inviting the audience to use their imaginations and embark on the journey right alongside the cast. Transforming ordinary items into something spectacular through intimate staging is an exciting challenge.

**JaxPlays: What aspects of the collaborative rehearsal process have you found most rewarding so far?**

**Billy:** Partnering with such a talented and enthusiastic ensemble has been deeply rewarding, particularly when exploring physical comedy. Their willingness to embrace my wild ideas makes every rehearsal exceptionally joyful.

Additionally, staging the production in the round brings the audience intimately close to the action, ensuring they don't miss any of the actors' subtle, nuanced performances.

**JaxPlays: In what ways are you collaborating with the design team to elevate and interpret the script spatially and aesthetically?**

**Billy:** I am so fortunate to be surrounded by such an immensely talented and dedicated creative team. Collaborating closely with our designers, we are building a highly adaptable world of Victorian London using versatile elements like trunks, crates and barrels.

Because our actors take on multiple roles and rely heavily on physical storytelling, we've crafted a minimalist yet deeply atmospheric environment where dynamic lighting and simple props can instantly transform the space, seamlessly supporting both the comedic beats and the emotional transitions of the story.

**JaxPlays: Which scene or moment in the production holds the most resonance for you personally?**

**Billy:** The scenes depicting Scrooge as a young boy and his poignant interaction with his mother are deeply moving. They beautifully capture the innocence of childhood and remind us of the lasting memories we all carry with us, making them especially powerful during the holiday season.
