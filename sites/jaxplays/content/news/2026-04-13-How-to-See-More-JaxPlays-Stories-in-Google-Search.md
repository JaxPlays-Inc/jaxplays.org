When you search Google for Jacksonville theatre news, auditions, reviews or weekend plans, the best local coverage is not always the first thing you see. National sites, AI summaries and thin event listings can crowd the page before you reach the reporting, calendars and context built specifically for Northeast Florida's theatre community.<!--more-->

Google now gives readers a little more control over that experience through Preferred Sources, a Search feature that lets you choose publications you want to see more often in news results.

For JaxPlays readers, that means a simple setting can make it easier for jaxplays.org stories to appear when you search for relevant local theatre topics.

## What Google Preferred Sources Does

[Google launched Preferred Sources](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/preferred-sources/) in the U.S. in August 2025 as a way for people to customize the news sources they see in Search.

When you add a publication, Google says articles from that source can appear more prominently in Top Stories when the publication has fresh, relevant coverage connected to your search. Google may also show a dedicated "From your sources" section in search results.

The setting does not remove other publications from your results. It simply tells Google that a source matters to you.

For a local theatre reader, that can help when you are searching for terms like "Jacksonville auditions," "Jacksonville theatre reviews," "shows opening this weekend" or the name of a local production, venue or company.

## Add JaxPlays the Quick Way

If you are already signed in to your Google account, the easiest path is this direct link:

[Add jaxplays.org as a preferred source on Google](https://www.google.com/preferences/source?q=jaxplays.org)

Search for `jaxplays.org`, then check the box next to JaxPlays or jaxplays.org when it appears. If Google asks you to sign in first, sign in and continue from there.

Once the setting is saved, it should follow your Google account across devices where you are signed in.

## Add JaxPlays From Google Search

You can also add JaxPlays from a normal Google search.

1. Go to [google.com](https://www.google.com/) and make sure you are signed in.
2. Search for a news topic, such as "Jacksonville theatre" or "Jacksonville auditions."
3. Scroll to the Top Stories section, if one appears.
4. Look for the source-preferences icon next to the Top Stories heading.
5. Click the icon, search for `jaxplays.org` and select JaxPlays or jaxplays.org.
6. Reload your results.

Google does not show the Top Stories box for every search, so the direct preference link is usually faster.

## Add JaxPlays From Google Settings

If you prefer to go through your Google account settings:

1. Go to [google.com](https://www.google.com/) and sign in.
2. Click your profile photo in the top-right corner.
3. Choose Search Personalization.
4. Open Source Preferences.
5. Search for `jaxplays.org` and select JaxPlays or jaxplays.org.

The exact labels can shift as Google updates Search, but the setting lives under Search personalization.

## Why It Helps

JaxPlays is built for people who care about live theatre in Northeast Florida and Southeast Georgia: audience members deciding what to see, performers watching for auditions, companies sharing announcements, artists tracking credits and readers looking for reviews and interviews.

Adding jaxplays.org as a preferred source helps Google understand that this local theatre coverage is something you want surfaced when it is relevant to your search. It is a small setting, but it can make a difference when local arts information is competing with broader web results.
