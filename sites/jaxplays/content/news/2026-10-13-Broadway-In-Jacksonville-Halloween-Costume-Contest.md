[[theatre:FSCJ Artist Series]] is inviting Broadway fans across Northeast Florida to turn Halloween into a Broadway spotlight moment with a costume contest tied to the 2026-2027 Broadway in Jacksonville season.<!--more-->

The Broadway in Jacksonville Halloween Costume Contest, sponsored by [City Grille & Raw Bar](https://citygrillerawbar.com/), asks participants to submit photos of costumes inspired by the season's five featured touring productions: [[production:2026 Hamilton|Hamilton]], [[production:2027 The Sound of Music|The Sound of Music]], [[production:2027 The Wiz|The Wiz]], [[production:2027 Jersey Boys|Jersey Boys]] and [[production:2027 The Outsiders|The Outsiders]].

Entries may be original designs, store-bought pieces or a mash-up drawing from the season lineup. Costume photos are due by 11:59 p.m. Thursday, Oct. 22, 2026.

A panel will select three finalists, who are expected to be notified by Oct. 24. The finalists will appear on WTLV's *First Coast Living* on Wednesday, Oct. 28, for a live costume showcase before local celebrity judges choose the grand-prize winner.

The grand prize includes two Broadway in Jacksonville subscriptions for the 2026-2027 season, a backstage tour of the national tour of *The Wiz* when it comes to Jacksonville, a $100 City Grille & Raw Bar gift card and a Broadway merchandise swag bag.

"We're thrilled to celebrate Halloween with a Broadway twist," Amanda Scott, FSCJ Artist Series assistant marketing director, said in the announcement. "This contest brings together creativity, community, and the excitement of our new season."

City Grille & Raw Bar, located at 1314 Prudential Drive in Jacksonville, is also offering Artist Series ticket holders 15% off dinner within seven days of a show ticketed through the FSCJ Artist Series, according to the announcement.

**Contest details**
- **What:** Broadway in Jacksonville Halloween Costume Contest
- **Who:** Open to the public
- **Deadline:** 11:59 p.m. Thursday, Oct. 22, 2026
- **Finalists:** Three finalists will be selected and notified by Oct. 24
- **Live showcase:** Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026, on WTLV's *First Coast Living*
- **Enter:** [Submit a costume entry](https://forms.gle/b6HSSNneggVdU3zn6)
- **Rules:** [Official contest rules and eligibility](https://www.fscjartistseries.org/media/4086/broadway-in-jacksonville-halloween-costume-contest-rules.pdf)
