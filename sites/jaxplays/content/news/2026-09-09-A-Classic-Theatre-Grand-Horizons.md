ST. AUGUSTINE, Fla. — The spotlight turns on this Friday at 7:30 p.m. as [[theatre:A Classic Theatre]] (ACT) opens its 2026-2027 season, serving up a razor-sharp blend of drama and comedy. Delivering high-impact productions with a professional edge, the company proudly stands behind its signature identity. "ACT operates with the attitude of being 'small but mighty,' embracing our role as a community theatre with a truly professional approach," notes Marketing Director Glo Macdonald.<!--more-->

The company's momentum comes backed by deep local roots. Established in 2007 by Jean Rahner and Anne Kraft — the original founders of [[theatre:Limelight Theatre]] — ACT navigates nearly two decades of evolution, expanding exponentially along the way. From its early nomadic roots to securing its permanent home at The Waterworks over the last five years, ACT continuously elevates the regional arts scene.

### Unbeatable Access

This year's marquee lineup showcases heavyweight talent: Tony Award and Pulitzer Prize winner [[Bess Wohl]] ([[production:2026 Grand Horizons]]), Tony Award winner [[Christopher Durang]] ([[production:2026 Vanya and Sonia and Masha and Spike]]), MacArthur Fellow and two-time Pulitzer Prize finalist [[Sarah Ruhl]] ([[production:2027 Dead Mans Cell Phone|Dead Man's Cell Phone]]) and PEN Center USA and Edgar Award winner [[Steven Dietz]] ([[production:2027 Beckys New Car|Becky's New Car]]).

Adding incredible prestige to the lineup, ACT brings work from celebrated playwright Bess Wohl — fresh off her historic 2026 Tony Award win for Best Play (and 2026 Pulitzer Prize for Drama) for her masterpiece *Liberation*. ACT is thrilled to present her hit, multigenerational comedy [[production:2026 Grand Horizons|Grand Horizons]] as a featured highlight of the new season. Directed by David Buchman, *Grand Horizons* — which earned a 2020 Tony Award nomination for Best Play — dives headfirst into family mechanics.

After 50 years of marriage, Bill and Nancy calmly announce their divorce at the Grand Horizons retirement community, stunning their adult sons, Brian and Ben. As the family descends into chaos, Ben's pregnant wife Jess and Brian's hookup Tommy are swept into the fray. With secrets unearthed and emotions unleashed, this sharp comedy turns a quiet retirement into a full-blown family reckoning.

### Grand Horizons Showtimes and Location

- **Dates:** Sept. 11, 12, 13, 18, 19 and 20, 2026
- **Schedule:** Fridays at 7:30 p.m., Saturdays at 4 p.m. and Sunday matinees at 2 p.m.
- **Venue:** The Waterworks, 184 San Marco Ave., St. Augustine, Fla.

"Our goal has always been to present plays that resonate with audiences long after the production ends," says Artistic Director Cindy Alexander. To keep high-caliber live performance accessible to everyone, ACT prioritizes keeping ticket prices low across the entire season.

Beyond the main stage, ACT expands its reach through its Staged Readings series at the Center for Spiritual Living. Selected from over 300 nationwide submissions, these one-night-only readings feature new scripts — giving local theatergoers a front-row seat to the industry's next breakout playwrights.

MainStage productions run across two action-packed weekends. The MainStage season kicks off this Friday. Reserve your seats: Visit [aclassictheatre.org](https://www.aclassictheatre.org) to grab tickets for opening night.
