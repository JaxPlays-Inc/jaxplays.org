Theatre serves to connect human emotion, foster community ties and promote education. Presented through a partnership between [[theatre:Arts Collective Theatre]] and [[venue:Ponte Vedra High School Auditorium|Ponte Vedra High School]], *[[production:2026 We Will Never Forget|9/11: We Will Never Forget]]* is written, directed and performed by actor, director and educator [[person:Jason Nettle]].<!--more-->

The solo performance brings together a local community arts organization and a secondary school to focus on collective memory and historical events. As producer [[person:Lee Hamby]] notes, "Theatre is not only meant to entertain - it also helps us remember, reflect, and connect."

Having shared the stage with Jason and his wife, Katie Nettle, in *Come From Away* at Alhambra Theatre & Dining - standing right beside him as our characters watched the towers fall on screen - I saw his generosity as a scene partner firsthand. Jason shares space and brings a deeply grounded authenticity to even the heaviest historical moments.

"As the 25th anniversary of September 11 approaches, an entire generation has grown up without personally experiencing that day," Hamby explains. "Jason Nettle has created a show that places the focus on the lives affected, the courage and humanity witnessed that day, and ensuring that we remember."

Originally written as his graduate thesis, *9/11: We Will Never Forget* draws from Nettle's personal experiences in New York and stories from survivors. In this solo piece, Nettle portrays 22 individuals to present multiple perspectives on the event. The performance runs 102 minutes, matching the time elapsed from the first plane's impact to the fall of the second tower.

What Nettle has crafted here functions as living digital history: a vital repository of human truth in an era where past events are easily flattened into archived footage and sound bites. While digital archives preserve facts, live performance translates those recorded artifacts back into shared human breath. By weaving primary accounts, survivor voices and a precise temporal structure into performance art, he builds an interactive archive that allows an audience to physically experience history together rather than passively scroll past it.

Nettle began acting at age 10, and his background includes regional and national productions, including national tours of *The Full Monty* and *Cabaret*, along with roles in television and film projects including *Law & Order*, *Sex and the City*, *What Would You Do?* and *Kate & Leopold*. He holds a BFA from Otterbein University and an MFA from the University of Central Florida, and was named 2020 Teacher of the Year for St. Johns County.

By bringing *9/11: We Will Never Forget* to the stage, Arts Collective Theatre and Ponte Vedra High School connect storytelling directly with Northeast Florida audiences by ensuring a dedicated space for remembrance, reflection and conversation. These local partnerships help ensure that the sacrifices and stories of that day continue to be shared with generations who were not there to experience it themselves.
