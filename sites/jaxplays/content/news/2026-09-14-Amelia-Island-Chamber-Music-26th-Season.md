The Amelia Island Chamber Music Festival will bring six-time Tony Award winner Audra McDonald to Nassau County this winter as part of its 26th season, a December-to-June lineup built around major classical artists, Broadway voices, jazz, bluegrass and community programming.<!--more-->

McDonald will perform "An Evening with Audra McDonald" on Feb. 23, 2027, at Amelia River Club. The concert is one of the season's marquee events, placing one of Broadway's most celebrated performers in the kind of close-range setting that has become central to the festival's identity.

Now entering its second quarter-century, the Amelia Island Chamber Music Festival continues to present artists in intimate venues rather than traditional concert halls, with concerts planned for churches, chapels, breweries, outdoor spaces and historic sites across Amelia Island.

"We are so thrilled to be bringing some of the best talent in the world to Amelia Island for our audiences, but I feel, more importantly, we are expanding access to great music for our community with our free community programming," Executive Director [[person:Robyn Marie Lamp]] said in the announcement. "There's something else that makes the Festival special: our intimate venues, where audiences of 350 or fewer are close enough to truly feel connected to the music and the artists."

### Season Highlights

The season opens Dec. 16, 2026, with "Sounds of the Season" featuring two-time GRAMMY winner Nicole Zuraitis and her quartet in the outdoor courtyard at the Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island.

The Dover Quartet returns Feb. 19, 2027, to [[venue:Amelia Plantation Chapel]], where the ensemble once served as the festival's quartet-in-residence. The festival will also present cellist Johannes Moser and pianist Marc-André Hamelin on March 2, 2027, in a Beethoven cello sonata cycle marking 200 years since the composer's death.

Balsam Range, a two-time International Bluegrass Music Association Entertainer of the Year, performs March 14, 2027, at the Ritz-Carlton, bringing bluegrass into the festival's genre-crossing mix.

GRAMMY-winning violinist Joshua Bell appears April 23, 2027, at [[venue:Amelia Plantation Chapel]] with soprano Larisa Martínez and pianist Peter Dugan in "Voice and the Violin." The season also includes "Beethoven & Beer" with the American String Quartet on June 9, 2027, at Mocama Beer Company, offering a casual, one-hour Beethoven program in a brewery setting.

Additional season events include Anderson & Roe, The Concert Truck, Curtis on Tour, the Festival Finale and the Marasco Young Artists' Concert.

### Community Programming

The festival's education and community work remains part of the 2026-2027 season, including the Christopher Rex String Seminar, in-school performances throughout Nassau County, public masterclasses with visiting artists, free outdoor performances by The Concert Truck and a Music and Mindfulness Initiative launched with Baptist Health.

This season also introduces a $20 rush ticket policy, offering available seats at a reduced rate shortly before each performance. Student tickets remain available for $15.
