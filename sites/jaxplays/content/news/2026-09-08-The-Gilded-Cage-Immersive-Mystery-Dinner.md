In New York City, traditional theatergoing has undergone a seismic shift. Audiences are no longer content simply sitting quietly in dark auditoriums separated by a stage wall. Instead, the cultural zeitgeist belongs entirely to immersive theatre — a movement supercharged by sensory-rich productions like [*Masquerade*](https://masqueradenyc.com/) and high-end nightlife hybrids where guests eat, drink and step straight into the world of the story.<!--more-->

Now, that high-concept Manhattan energy is landing right here in Northeast Florida. [[theatre:Catalyst Theatre]] has announced [[production:2026 The Gilded Cage: An Immersive Mystery Dinner]], bringing a lavish 1928-themed speakeasy supper club experience to The Venue in Hastings. Just like its New York inspirations, *The Gilded Cage* breaks down the barrier between performer and audience, merging local fine dining with interactive, real-time drama.

While traditional dinner theatre often relies on pre-recorded tracks or familiar scripts, *The Gilded Cage* stands out for its commitment to original storytelling and a professional creative and performance team. The production brings together an ensemble of nine regional actors alongside a live four-piece band performing period-inspired musical arrangements that shift dynamically around the dining room as the plot unfolds.

Set inside a Prohibition-era speakeasy, guests step into a murder mystery where every table is seated at the center of the narrative. Whispered secrets, dramatic showdowns and hidden clues take place inches from guests' plates as they navigate the evening.

"In major arts capitals like NYC, audiences want experiences, not just performances," says Sherrie Provence, Artistic Director at Catalyst Theatre. "With *The Gilded Cage*, we're pairing original theatrical storytelling and live musicianship with incredible local culinary artists. It's an evening designed around genuine connection — bringing people back to the same physical table to share something entirely new."

### Elevating the Regional Arts & Culinary Scene

The production isn't just bringing a national cultural trend to Northeast Florida; it's also acting as a showcase for local culinary talent and community revitalization. Each performance pairs the narrative action with a multicourse gourmet menu curated by standout regional chefs:

- Chef Matthew Brown headlines the kitchen for the Oct. 2 and 3 performances.
- Chef Ellie Shultze takes over culinary direction for the Oct. 30 performance.

Adding to the event's local roots, a portion of all ticket proceeds directly benefits Hastings Main Street, supporting historic preservation and community economic development.

### Event Details & Reservations

With immersive supper club events routinely selling out months in advance in major metropolitan markets, capacity for *The Gilded Cage* remains strictly limited to preserve the intimate, interactive atmosphere.

**Event details**
- **Dates:** Oct. 2, Oct. 3 and Oct. 30, 2026
- **Time:** Cocktail reception begins at 6 p.m.
- **Location:** The Venue, 301 N. Main St., Hastings, Fla.
