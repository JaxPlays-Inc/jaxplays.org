Written by [[person:Niveah Glover]] and directed by [[person:James F. Webb III]], *Callas* centers on the Brown sisters in a Brooklyn brownstone in 1970. In the wake of their mother's death, Margretta, June and Sylvia navigate grief, funeral plans, family history and uncertain futures.

The production debuted Aug. 1-3, 2025, at JAMS Black Box Theatre in Jacksonville.
