*Little Women* is a timeless classic by Louisa May Alcott about holding onto the dear things that matter most and finding extraordinary meaning in ordinary lives. In an uncertain world shaped by intense hardship and change, the March sisters fill their humble home with warmth, light, wild imagination, and unwavering love. As Meg, Jo, Beth, and Amy journey from girlhood into becoming little women, they navigate the joys, challenges, and bittersweet realities of growing up.
