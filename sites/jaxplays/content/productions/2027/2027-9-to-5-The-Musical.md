Inspired by the hit movie and featuring an irresistible score by Dolly Parton, *9 to 5* follows three fed-up coworkers who turn the tables on their sexist, egotistical boss. Big laughs, great music and plenty of girl power make this workplace comedy a crowd-pleasing blast.
