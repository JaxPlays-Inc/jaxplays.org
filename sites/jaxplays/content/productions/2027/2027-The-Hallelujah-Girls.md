When a group of spirited Southern women takes over an abandoned church and turns it into a day spa, the result is friendship, romance and plenty of hilarious small-town chaos. From the writers of *The Savannah Sipping Society*, *The Hallelujah Girls* is a warmhearted comedy about changing your life before it changes you.
