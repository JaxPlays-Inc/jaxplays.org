When struggling actor Michael Dorsey cannot land a job, he creates one by transforming himself into actress Dorothy Michaels. Suddenly he is a Broadway sensation, and his life becomes hilariously complicated. Based on the beloved film, *Tootsie* is a wildly funny, fast-paced musical comedy about ambition, love and one spectacularly elaborate disguise.
