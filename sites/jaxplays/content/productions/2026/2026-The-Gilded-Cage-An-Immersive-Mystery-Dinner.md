Step inside a 1928 supper club where dinner and theatre unfold together.

Catalyst Theatre presents *The Gilded Cage: An Immersive Mystery Dinner* in partnership with [[venue:The Venue-Hastings]]. Each performance begins at 6 p.m. with a cocktail reception and passed hors d'oeuvres, followed by a three-course dinner and a live theatrical production filled with clues, secrets and unexpected twists.

Chef Matthew Brown prepares the culinary experience for the Oct. 2 and 3 performances, while Chef Ellie Shultze leads the dinner for the Oct. 30 performance. Tickets are $125 per person, and advance reservations are required.
