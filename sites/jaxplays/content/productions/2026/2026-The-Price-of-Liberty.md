Set during the American Revolution, *The Price of Liberty* follows patriot and loyalist families as the war reshapes neighbors, siblings and households. Written by Louise Everett and directed by [[person:Patty Katez Everett|Patty Everett]], the Theatre Troupe 1776 production honors America's 250th anniversary by focusing on the ordinary people who carried the conflict's human cost.
