This Halloween season, All the Devils Shakespeare brings the ultimate vintage sci-fi thriller to life with a staged production of the infamous 1938 radio script of *The War of the Worlds*. Licensed through Concord Theatricals and keeping strictly to the original Howard Koch adaptation made famous by Orson Welles, this production drops audiences right into a 1930s-style broadcasting studio.

Audiences will experience the gripping "breaking news" format that simulated a real-time Martian invasion, complete with an ensemble cast playing frantic field reporters, desperate military officials and a live team crafting old-school foley sound effects in front of them. It is a fast-paced, high-stakes theatrical event that captures the spooky, mounting dread of the original broadcast.
