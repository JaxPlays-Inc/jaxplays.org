*The Elixir of Love* follows Nemorino, a shy young man hopelessly in love with the confident Adina. When a traveling salesman offers him a supposed magic love potion, Nemorino takes a chance on winning her heart. What follows is a funny, heartfelt story filled with misunderstandings, memorable music, and unexpected romance.

Sung in Italian with English surtitles.
