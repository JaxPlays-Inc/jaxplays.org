Wednesday Addams has fallen in love, and one dinner with the Beinekes is all it takes to send both families into a night of secrets, surprises, and deliciously dark comedy.

The Island Theater brings *The Addams Family* to the stage with a musical celebration of love, loyalty, and the wonderfully strange ties that hold a family together.
