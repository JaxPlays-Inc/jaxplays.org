Presented in partnership by [[theatre:Arts Collective Theatre]] and [[venue:Ponte Vedra High School Auditorium|Ponte Vedra High School]], *9/11: We Will Never Forget* is a solo performance written, directed and performed by [[person:Jason Nettle]].

The piece draws from Nettle's personal experiences in New York and the stories of fellow survivors. The performance runs 102 minutes, honoring the span of time from the first plane's impact to the fall of the final tower, and gives voice to 22 distinct individuals through one performer.

The production plays at [[venue:Arts Collective Theatre]] on Wednesday, Sept. 9, 2026, and at [[venue:Ponte Vedra High School Auditorium]] on Thursday, Sept. 10, 2026.
