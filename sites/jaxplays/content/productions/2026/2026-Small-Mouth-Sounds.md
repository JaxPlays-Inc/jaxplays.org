Set at a quiet retreat in upstate New York, *Small Mouth Sounds* follows six urban runaways seeking sanctuary, clarity and healing. Guided by a disembodied teacher, the characters navigate personal grief and desperation under a strict vow of silence.

Jacksonville University's 2026 production is directed by Thaddeus Walker and runs Oct. 22-24 at Swisher Theater.
