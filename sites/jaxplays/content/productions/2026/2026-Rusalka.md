*Rusalka* follows a water nymph who longs to become human after falling in love with a prince. Against her father Vodník's warnings, she turns to the witch Ježibaba, who grants the transformation at a devastating cost: Rusalka loses her voice, and betrayal will curse both her and the prince.

When the prince brings Rusalka into the human world, her silence and strange behavior isolate her, while a Foreign Princess draws the prince away. Rejected and unable to return fully to her former life, Rusalka becomes bound to a tragic fate. In the final act, she refuses Ježibaba's offer to save herself by killing the prince, choosing mercy even as the prince seeks the fatal kiss that seals the opera's ending.
