*I Love You, You're Perfect... Now Sign Here* is a world-premiere romantic comedy by Jacksonville playwright [[person:David Sacks]].

When Hannah, from a wealthy conservative Jewish family in Greenwich, Connecticut, and Michael, from a blue-collar Irish family in Brooklyn, travel toward marriage, their relationship meets the expectations, anxieties and paperwork that come with bringing two families together.
