Produced by Midnight Creative, *Edgar Allan Poe: The Musical* brings Poe's gothic imagination to life at Friday Musicale Inc in Jacksonville.

The 90-minute immersive experience combines live storytelling, theatrical music and themed cocktails as a dark host guides audiences through reimagined works including "The Tell-Tale Heart," "The Raven," "The Masque of the Red Death" and "The Cask of Amontillado."
