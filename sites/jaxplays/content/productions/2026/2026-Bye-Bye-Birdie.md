It is 1958, and rock-and-roll idol Conrad Birdie has been drafted into the U.S. Army. His agent, Albert Peterson, and Albert's secretary and girlfriend, Rose Alvarez, plan one final publicity stunt: Birdie will give a farewell kiss to fan Kim MacAfee on *The Ed Sullivan Show* before reporting for duty.

Birdie's arrival turns Kim's hometown of Sweet Apple, Ohio, upside down as teenagers swoon, parents panic, and Albert and Rose try to keep the spectacle—and their relationship—from spinning out of control.

**Content advisory:** General audiences.
