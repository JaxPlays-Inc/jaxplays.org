Amelia Island Opera presents *A Night at the Sock-Hopera*, a 1950s-themed event pairing Gian Carlo Menotti's *Old Maid and the Thief* with songs from the era.

The cast includes McKenna Slack as Laetitia, Emily Geller as Miss Todd, Jesse Malgieri as Bob and Robyn Marie Lamp as Miss Pinkerton, with Bethany Griffin, John Tibbetts and Victoria Isernia featured as soloists. Carroll Freeman directs, with Arthur Bosarge serving as musical director and accompanist.
