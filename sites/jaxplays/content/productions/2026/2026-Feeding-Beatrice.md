In *Feeding Beatrice*, a young Black couple moves into a crumbling mansion they hope can become their first home. The suspiciously low price soon reveals a more sinister cost when Beatrice, a white woman from another era, begins haunting the house and shaping the couple's lives.

Players by the Sea presents the gothic horror and social satire on its Studio Stage. Seating is open, with subscriber seats reserved, and the theatre advises mature audiences only.
